## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** Tech recruiters, engineering hiring managers, and prospective freelance clients seeking full-stack engineers with strong creative frontend craft and data science / Power BI expertise.
- **Secondary:** Technical collaborators, designers, and developer community peers evaluating open-source contributions and interactive web implementations.

## Product Purpose

Serve as Ayush Singh's primary portfolio, proof-of-work archive, and client acquisition platform—presenting a cohesive narrative that unites full-stack engineering, interactive creative technology, and data intelligence.

## Positioning

A unique convergence of **Full-Stack Engineering & Creative Technology** with **Data Science & Predictive Business Intelligence (Power BI, Python, FastAPI)**. Rather than choosing between analytical depth and visual polish, the portfolio demonstrates mastery in both: high-performance, responsive digital systems paired with data architecture and interactive storytelling.

## Operating Context

- Browsed on modern desktop and mobile browsers, frequently evaluated in fast 15-to-30 second screening passes by recruiters or prospective clients.
- Visitors expect immediate access to live production links, GitHub repositories, verifiable technical stacks, and frictionless contact channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities:** Interactive project showcases, filterable/categorized skill matrices, Canva visual design gallery, dedicated resume view, and interactive contact modal with validation.
- **Constraints:** Preserve verified project records (AI Creator Hub, PulseAnalytics, etc.), actual contact information, and confirmed technical stacks. Avoid fabricating testimonials, awards, or fake metrics.

## Product Principles

1. **Lead with Proof and Craft:** Let live demonstrations, clean typography, and responsive interactions speak before claims.
2. **Harmonize Data and Design:** Give equal weight to analytical rigor (Power BI, predictive models) and aesthetic precision (interactive 3D, micro-animations).
3. **Performance First:** Keep rendering silky (60fps), transitions instant, and layout shifts at zero.
4. **Frictionless Action:** Every screen must clearly guide visitors toward live demos, code inspection, or initiating contact in one click.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adhere to WCAG AA color contrast standards across dark and high-contrast modes.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for complex canvas/3D or intensive parallax animations.
- Ensure semantic landmark regions, keyboard focus indicators, and accessible form labels.
